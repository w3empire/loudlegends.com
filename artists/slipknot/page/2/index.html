Slipknot

Nu metal Heavy metal Metal
Years Active: 1995 – Present (31 years)

Slipknot is a metal band from Iowa known for its nine masked members and loud, fast songs. They mix heavy guitars, sharp drums, and harsh vocals with a chaotic stage show. Formed in 1995, the group rose quickly with albums like Iowa and Vol. 3. Their music explores anger, pain, and identity through noise and rhythm.

📍 Des Moines, Iowa, USA

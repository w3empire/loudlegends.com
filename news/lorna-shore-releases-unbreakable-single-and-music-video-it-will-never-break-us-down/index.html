LORNA SHORE Releases ‘Unbreakable’ Single and Music Video: ‘It Will Never Break Us Down’

By Tobias Romero
Jun 26, 2025

Lorna Shore has dropped a new single, “Unbreakable”, along with a fresh music video, giving fans another preview of their next album, I Feel The Everblack Festering Within Me.

The video, directed by Eric Richter, is available to watch below. A new line of merchandise is also out now and can be found here.

Vocalist Will Ramos shared that the song is about resilience and unity. “It’s a song that’s meant to bring people together,” Ramos said. “No matter what the world throws at us, we’ll keep standing. We’re unbreakable.”

The track follows their previous release, “Oblivion,” which sparked plenty of buzz in recent weeks. Both songs are part of the band’s upcoming album, their fifth studio release, set to arrive September 12 through Century Media Records.

It’s their first new album since 2022’s Pain Remains.

Lorna Shore’s current lineup includes Will Ramos (vocals), Adam De Micco (lead guitar), Andrew O’Connor (rhythm guitar), Michael Yager (bass), and Austin Archey (drums). Over the last few years, the band has gained attention with viral success on Spotify, massive streaming numbers, and tours that have helped grow their audience in extreme metal circles.

Their sound blends brutal riffs, intense drumming, and a flair for theatrical melodies, pushing against genre norms while building a distinct identity. This new album looks to continue that trend.

I Feel The Everblack Festering Within Me will be released in multiple formats, including digital and a wide variety of vinyl editions. Some versions are highly limited and tied to specific retailers or tour sales. A few highlights:

  • Digital version on all platforms
  • CD editions, including a limited O-Card version
  • Vinyl variants: golden, black, sea blue smoke, magenta, and more
  • Several special editions limited to 300 to 1,000 units each

Upcoming Tour

Lorna Shore will take the new songs on the road this fall, joined by The Black Dahlia Murder, Shadow Of Intent, and PeelingFlesh.

The tour kicks off September 17 in Pittsburgh and wraps up October 30 in New York City. Some festival appearances are also included.

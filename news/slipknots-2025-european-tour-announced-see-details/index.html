Slipknot’s 2025 European Tour Announced; See Details

Tickets for select dates available starting November 8.

By Griffin Kozlowski
Nov 5, 2024
Summary
  • Slipknot announces 2025 European Tour, kicking off at Sweden Rock Festival and wrapping up at Resurrection Fest in Spain.
  • The tour features headlining shows in Germany and France, alongside appearances at major festivals like Rock Am Ring and Copenhell.
  • Tickets for select dates go on sale Friday, November 8, so fans better brace for the rush.

The masters of mayhem in terrifying masks are set to invade Europe again. Slipknot—yes, the band that makes mosh pits feel like a full-contact sport—dropped the news on Instagram about their upcoming 2025 tour. This isn’t their first round of European chaos, with additional dates already planned for December. Apparently, they’ve decided you can’t have too much of a good thing—or too much headbanging.

The schedule is loaded with massive festivals that make regular gigs look like karaoke night. Sweden Rock Festival kicks things off, followed by appearances at Germany’s Rock Im Park and Rock Am Ring, among others. If you’re more into obscure venues than festival chaos, a few headlining dates in Germany and France might tempt you. No word yet on support acts, but hey, maybe that Slipknot tribute band from Shell Shock II has cleared their schedule. You know, the one that got booked when half the actual lineup bailed? What a moment.

Highlights include stops at Copenhell in Denmark, Nova Rock in Austria, and the gritty Resurrection Fest in Spain to close out the madness. For fans in France and Germany, standalone shows in cities like Lyon and Berlin offer a chance to avoid the mud-soaked festival crowds—though good luck avoiding sweaty moshers.

Tickets for the standalone shows and one lucky festival go on sale Friday, November 8. Start refreshing your browser now because those hardcore fans will probably have their bots locked and loaded.

Full List of 2025 European Dates:

  • 04.6 – Sölvesborg, SE – @swedenrockfestival
  • 06.6 – Nuremberg, DE – @rockimparkofficial
  • 07.6 – Nürburg, DE – @rockamringofficial
  • 08.6 – Nancy, FR – @heavyweekend
  • 10.6 – Hannover, DE – Expo Plaza*
  • 13.6 – Nickelsdorf, AT – @novarockfestival
  • 14.6 – Interlaken, CH – @greenfieldfestival
  • 17.6 – Ferrara, IT – @ferrarasummerfestival*
  • 21.6 – Copenhagen, DK – @copenhell
  • 23.6 – Berlin, DE – Waldbühne*
  • 25.6 – Lyon, FR – LDLC Arena*
  • 26.6 – Barcelona, ES – @bcnrockfest
  • 28.6 – Viveiro, ES – @resurrectionfest

*Tickets On Sale Friday, November 8

Better get ready, folks. Slipknot isn’t just bringing the noise—they’re bringing their entire chaotic circus back to Europe, oldschool style. Hope your neck muscles are prepared.

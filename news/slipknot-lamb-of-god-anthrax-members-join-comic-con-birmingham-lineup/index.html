Slipknot, Lamb of God, Anthrax Members Join Comic Con Birmingham Lineup

By Magnus Ferreira
Jun 30, 2025

Comic Con is coming to Birmingham, and this year’s event will feature more than just movie and TV stars. Several well-known rock and metal musicians are set to appear, including members of Slipknot, Lamb of God, and Anthrax.

Comic Con Midlands (Birmingham) will be held for the first time on July 11-12 at the NEC. The event is organized by Monopoly Events, the company behind popular shows like Comic Con Liverpool, Comic Con Edinburgh, and For The Love Of Horror.

Music fans will get the chance to meet:

  • Sid Wilson of Slipknot
  • Randy Blythe of Lamb of God
  • Scott Ian and Frank Bello of Anthrax

The event will also feature appearances by the Osbourne family, Ozzy, Sharon, Kelly, and Jack, adding to the mix of music and pop culture.

In addition to musicians, the guest list includes actors from popular shows and games, such as:

  • The Vampire Diaries
  • The Originals
  • Arrow
  • Teen Wolf
  • Twilight
  • Red Dead Redemption 2
  • Deadpool & Wolverine (including Peggy, also known as Dogpool)

Comic Con Midlands is expected to include a variety of attractions:

  • Photo ops and autographs with celebrity guests
  • Panels and Q&As with actors and musicians
  • Workshops on cosplay, comic writing, and special effects
  • A large exhibitor hall with collectibles, comics, and merchandise
  • An Artist Alley featuring one-of-a-kind artwork
  • A gaming zone with video games, tabletop RPGs, and more
  • Cosplay contests and opportunities to show off fan costumes
  • Family activities like kids’ cosplay and drawing sessions

Andy Kleek, CEO of Monopoly Events, said: “We’ve had our eye on Birmingham for a while. It has such a vibrant, passionate community of fans and we can’t wait to host a Comic Con there.”

Organizers say they expect thousands of attendees and aim to deliver a welcoming and exciting experience for fans of all ages.

For full details on guests and event information, visit the official Comic Con Midlands website.

About Comic Con Midlands

Comic Con Midlands is a new fan convention set to debut in Birmingham on July 11-12 at the NEC. Created by Monopoly Events, the company known for organizing large-scale pop culture shows across the UK, this event brings their signature format to England’s second-largest city for the first time.

The convention is designed to appeal to a broad audience, offering something for longtime fans, families, casual attendees, and collectors. It blends elements of film, TV, music, gaming, comics, and cosplay into one weekend.

Unlike some niche conventions, Comic Con Midlands mixes mainstream favorites with cult hits. Attendees can expect photo sessions, autograph opportunities, fan panels, live Q&As, interactive workshops, and a variety of shopping options.

The venue, NEC Birmingham, provides a spacious layout to accommodate multiple zones, including the Exhibitor Hall, Artist Alley, Gaming Zone, and designated areas for cosplay and family activities.

Comic Con Midlands joins a growing portfolio that includes Comic Con Liverpool, Comic Con Edinburgh, and themed events like For the Love of Horror. These shows regularly draw crowds in the tens of thousands, thanks to guest appearances from actors, musicians, and pop culture icons.

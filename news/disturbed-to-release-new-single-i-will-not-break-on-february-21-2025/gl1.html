Did you know? Disturbed's frontman, David Draiman, was almost a rabbi before choosing a career in metal. He studied at several Jewish schools and even spent time in a religious seminary in Israel. But after realizing the rockstar life was more his speed, he ditched the rabbinical path and went on to create some of the heaviest anthems in modern metal.

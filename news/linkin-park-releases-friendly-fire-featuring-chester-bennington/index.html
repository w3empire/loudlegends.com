Linkin Park Releases "Friendly Fire" Featuring Chester Bennington

New Track Joins Papercuts (Singles Collection 2000–2023) Releasing April 12.

By Levi Morel
Feb 23, 2024
Summary
  • Linkin Park has released the previously unheard track ‘Friendly Fire’, featuring the late Chester Bennington.
  • Greatest hits album titled Papercuts (Singles Collection 2000–2023) will be released on April 12 via Warner.
  • The album includes 20 tracks, spanning hits like ‘One Step Closer’ and rare gems such as ‘QWERTY’.

Art has a way of keeping artists alive, doesn’t it? Even when they’re gone, their work lingers on, creating an odd form of immortality. Linkin Park just gave fans a bittersweet reminder of that idea by releasing “Friendly Fire”, an unreleased track featuring the unmistakable vocals of the late Chester Bennington.

This release isn’t a standalone gesture. It’s part of the band’s announcement of their upcoming greatest hits album, Papercuts (Singles Collection 2000–2023), set to drop on April 12 under Warner. A retrospective project with a bit of an emotional punch, if you ask me.

“Friendly Fire”, recorded during the One More Light sessions in 2017, has been sitting in the vault for years. Guitarist Brad Delson shared that the song was a band favorite but didn’t quite fit the final album. Now, with a few tweaks, it’s finally out there—and let’s be honest, this track would have torn up the pop/rock airwaves back in the day.

“‘Friendly Fire’ was always one of our favorites from the One More Light sessions. We loved it, but something didn’t feel quite right, so we held it back. Revisiting it for this collection, I was floored by its power—the storytelling, Chester’s vocal delivery, the sonic layers. It was closer to finished than we’d realized. We just needed to connect a few dots that were missing at the time.”

“It’s a beautiful, heartbreaking, hopeful song. I can’t wait for people to hear it.”

Papercuts promises 20 tracks covering the band’s legacy, from “One Step Closer” to deep cuts like “QWERTY”. According to co-founder Mike Shinoda, compiling the collection brought a flood of memories.

“Curating the tracks for Papercuts was pure nostalgia. Each song represents a moment in time, both for us and for the fans. From our debut single to the new ‘Friendly Fire,’ this tracklist tells our story. Thank you for being part of this journey—we hope you enjoy this collection as much as we enjoyed creating it.”

Although Linkin Park never officially disbanded, the group has been on hiatus since Bennington’s tragic death in 2017. Papercuts (Singles Collection 2000–2023) arrives April 12, carrying both the weight of the past and the hope of reconnecting with fans.

Tracklist for Papercuts:

  1. Crawling
  2. Faint
  3. Numb/Encore
  4. Papercut
  5. Breaking The Habit
  6. In The End
  7. Bleed It Out
  8. Somewhere I Belong
  9. Waiting For The End
  10. Castle Of Glass
  11. One More Light
  12. Burn It Down
  13. What I’ve Done
  14. QWERTY
  15. One Step Closer
  16. New Divide
  17. Leave Out All The Rest
  18. Lost
  19. Numb
  20. Friendly Fire

There you go folks. Linkin Park’s way of saying, “We’re not done yet.”

Linkin Park Announces From Zero Listening Events Across U.S.

Over 100 record stores will host exclusive previews before the album drops on November 15.

By Orion Duarte
Oct 31, 2024
Summary
  • Linkin Park will host listening events for their new album From Zero on Saturday, November 9, at over 100 U.S. record stores.
  • The album officially drops on Friday, November 15, but fans can preorder it now for early access.
  • International events are coming soon, with stores like Crash Records in Leeds and Rough Trade in London already on board.

Linkin Park is at it again, boys and girls—this time with listening events scattered across over 100 U.S. record stores for their new album, From Zero. Scheduled for Saturday, November 9, these events give fans a sneak peek at the record before it officially hits shelves on Friday, November 15. Yeah, a full six days to figure out if the “revitalized” Linkin Park is worth all the noise—or if you’ll be needing earplugs.

This whole affair comes with its share of lingering tension. The band’s recent return hasn’t exactly been a smooth ride. Between “allegations” and family drama—yeah, Chester’s mom’s been vocal about how desperately they’re trying to “erase the past”—there’s been more chaos than clarity. Still, if you’re the curious type or just loyal to a fault, these listening parties might scratch that itch.

And for the international crowd, sit tight. Linkin Park says they’ll announce non-U.S. events soon. Some stores, like Crash Records in Leeds and a couple of Rough Trade locations in London, have already jumped the gun and put themselves on the map.

When From Zero finally drops on November 15, you’ll be able to decide for yourself whether it’s a fresh start or just another sad trip down nostalgia lane. If you’re already sold, you can preorder the album right here.

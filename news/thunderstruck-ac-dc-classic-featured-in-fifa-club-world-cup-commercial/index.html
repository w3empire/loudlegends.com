"Thunderstruck," AC/DC Classic, Featured in FIFA Club World Cup Commercial

By Griffin Kozlowski
Jun 17, 2025

AC/DC’s Thunderstruck is the soundtrack for Budweiser’s new commercial.

The beer company is a sponsor of the FIFA Club World Cup, which features 32 teams and is currently being held in the United States.

Released on June 6, the Budweiser ad has already surpassed 80,000 views. You can watch the commercial by clicking the player below.

Thunderstruck is part of the album The Razors Edge, which came out in September 1990. The track, whose guitar riff is considered challenging even by its creator, the legendary Angus Young, has racked up over 1.8 billion streams on Spotify.

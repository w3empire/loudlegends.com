AC/DC Reportedly Planning Final Concerts For Late 2025 In Australia

The iconic rock band is expected to wrap up their legendary career with farewell shows in Melbourne and additional dates across Australia by year’s end.

By Magnus Ferreira
Jun 16, 2025

According to behind-the-scenes information obtained by Blunt magazine, AC/DC is expected to announce what could be the final concerts of their career, scheduled for late 2025 (Get AC/DC Tickets Here).

The band would bring their story full circle by wrapping things up in Australia, where it all began back in 1973.

Two shows are reportedly set for November 12 and 16 in Melbourne. The venue would be the Melbourne Cricket Ground, a stadium that holds over 100,000 people.

The plan includes adding more cities, with the final leg of the tour likely concluding in December.

On June 26, the Angus Young-led group will kick off a European tour that runs through August 21, ending in Edinburgh, Scotland. That location carries some symbolism, as it’s where Angus and his family originally lived before moving to Australia.

The current lineup still features one more classic member, vocalist Brian Johnson, who’s been with the band since 1980. Joining him are guitarist Stevie Young, Angus’s nephew who replaced the late Malcolm Young in 2017, bassist Chris Chaney, and drummer Matt Laug.

A Quick Look At AC/DC’s Career

AC/DC is one of rock’s most enduring and influential bands, with a career spanning over five decades. Formed in Sydney, Australia, in 1973 by brothers Angus and Malcolm Young, the group quickly became known for their raw, riff-heavy sound and no-frills approach to hard rock.

Their 1980 album Back in Black, recorded after the death of original singer Bon Scott, remains one of the best-selling albums in history, with Brian Johnson taking over on vocals.

Throughout the years, the band has stuck to a simple formula: loud guitars, pounding drums, and songs built for massive arenas. Tracks like “Highway to Hell,” “Thunderstruck,” and “You Shook Me All Night Long” have become staples of rock radio and sports arenas worldwide.

Despite lineup changes, personal losses, and health challenges, AC/DC has managed to keep their signature sound intact, becoming a symbol of rock consistency.

With over 200 million albums sold globally, they remain one of the most commercially successful rock bands ever.
